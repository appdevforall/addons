Share Your Apps

Publish your apps and their source to a store on your local network — no internet, no accounts

Version 1.0.2 · Author: App Dev For All · Package: org.appdevforall.shareyourapps

Executive overview

Share Your Apps turns the apps you build in Code on the Go into a small web store. People on the same Wi-Fi network or hotspot open it in their phone's browser and download each app's APK, its source, or both. Nothing goes through the internet.

The store is served by the companion Share Your Apps Server app, which the plugin carries, installs and starts.

Core functionality

  • Manage the catalog in the Share Your Apps tab: the store's name, description, header image and optional PIN, and products with their project, APK, pictures, videos and description. Everything is saved as you edit it.
  • Publish builds a complete new store beside the current one, starts the server on it, checks every file is served correctly, and only then removes the old one. If anything fails, the store keeps serving the previous version.
  • Templates. The store page is rendered with Pebble. Pick one of the built-in looks, or write your own.
  • Copy a store to another phone by QR code, a typed code, or sound — useful when a phone's camera is broken.
  • Help everywhere. Long-press any heading or button for a tooltip.

Technical architecture

Share Your Apps is two parts:

  • The plugin runs inside Code on the Go as a tab in the bottom sheet. It keeps the catalog in the plugin's private folder, renders the store page with Pebble templates, and on Publish writes a complete new Store_* folder to shared storage: each product's APK and its source as a zip, without build output, IDE settings or signing files.
  • Share Your Apps Server is a small Android app the plugin carries and installs. It serves the store folder over HTTP on Wi-Fi or a hotspot, as a foreground service, with an optional PIN. It also copies a store to another phone, encrypted, after the owner allows it, and can be controlled by other apps on the phone through an MCP endpoint on 127.0.0.1 that needs a token.

The plugin starts the server on the new folder, then downloads every file back from it over 127.0.0.1 and compares sizes and checksums before removing the old folder.

Usage

  1. Install the plugin and open the Share Your Apps tab.
  2. Name your store and add a product for each app you want to share.
  3. Tap Publish. The first time, the plugin installs Share Your Apps Server.
  4. Share the address the server shows. Anyone on the same network can open it.

Key benefits

  • Works offline. No internet, app store account or computer is needed to hand out an app.
  • Source and app together. Learners can download what a classmate built and how they built it.
  • Safe to update. Buyers keep the current store until the new one is checked and serving.
  • Passes on. A store can be copied to another phone, so it reaches people beyond one Wi-Fi network.
