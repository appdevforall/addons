Share Your Apps

Share Your Apps shares the apps you build in Code On The Go with people nearby — no internet, and no accounts. You describe your apps in the Share Your Apps tab, and tap Publish. The plugin builds a small website of them, and the Local Store Server app serves it over Wi-Fi or a hotspot, where buyers open it in their phone's browser.

Topics

  • The store: its name, description, header picture and PIN
  • Products: each app, its project, APK and source, and what buyers read
  • Pictures and videos, with descriptions and captions
  • Templates: the store page's look, and Extra JSON
  • Publishing: what happens, and what its messages mean

Local Store Server has its own help: long-press anything in it.
