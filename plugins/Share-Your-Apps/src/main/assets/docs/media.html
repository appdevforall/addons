Pictures and videos

Adding them

+ Add media adds pictures, videos or sounds. Buyers swipe through them on the product's card. Publishing copies them into the store, and makes a still frame for each video.

Describing them

Describe says what an item shows, for visually-impaired people, whose screen readers read it aloud in its place. Say what matters for choosing the app — The chat screen, with two messages — not what kind of item it is. Without one, the page says only which it is: Termux, picture 1 of 3.

Captions for a video

In a video's Describe, Choose captions file picks a WebVTT file, ending .vtt: text with the time each line appears. Buyers turn captions on in the video's menu, or its CC button. No captions removes them.

Removing one

Remove takes an item out of the product at your next publish, with its description and captions. The file itself stays on your phone.
