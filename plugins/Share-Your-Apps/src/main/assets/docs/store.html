The store

Store name

The name at the top of the store page, and in its browser tab.

Store description

Optional text under the name, in HTML. A tag opened but never closed, or similar, shows a red warning under the field; it doesn't stop you publishing.

Header image

An optional picture or short video across the top. A video shows a still frame, its poster, and plays when a buyer taps it, so buyers don't each download it on every visit.

PIN

Up to 6 digits, which buyers enter before reaching the store; leave it empty for none. It keeps casual visitors out, but isn't real security: the store doesn't use encryption.
