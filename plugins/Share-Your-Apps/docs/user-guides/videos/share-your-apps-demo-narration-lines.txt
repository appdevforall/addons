Share Your Apps lets you share the Android apps you build in Code On The Go with people nearby. No internet, no app store, and no accounts.
It has two parts. The Share Your Apps plugin, where you describe your apps and publish them. And the Local Store Server app, which serves them over Wi-Fi, or your phone's hotspot.
First, creating your store.
Install the plugin from Code On The Go's Addons Manager.
In the Share Your Apps tab, give your store a name. A description, a header picture and a PIN are optional.
Add a product for each app. Each card shows what buyers can download.
Choose the app's project, and the APK that buyers will install.
Add screenshots and videos, so buyers can see what they're getting.
Describe each picture, for buyers who use a screen reader.
Then tap Publish, and confirm.
The first time, Android asks you to allow a few things. Each one is needed just once.
Choose a template to change the store's look, or write your own.
When it's done, every file has been checked. And the store is already being served.
Next, letting people in.
Local Store Server shows what it's serving, and where.
Tap Show QR codes for buyers. On a hotspot, the first code joins its network, and the second opens the store.
With no Wi-Fi around, the server makes a hotspot of its own, with a name and password you choose.
Buyers see your store in their browser.
Each app has its pictures, its description, and its downloads. One tap, and it's on their phone.
And every store has an accessible version, plain and in large text.
Keep serving when the screen is off, or pause to save battery.
Finally, sharing your store with another phone.
Someone nearby can serve a copy of your store from their own phone. Tap Share this store.
Their phone scans the large code. The small one lets a phone without the app download it first.
On their phone, they tap Import a store, and scan the code, or type it.
Both phones show the same four digits, so you know you're sharing with the phone in front of you.
You choose when their copy expires, and tap Allow.
The copy arrives, and they tap Serve it now.
They can set their own PIN. But they can't change the store, or pass it on.
They let people in the same way, with codes of their own.
And on the date you chose, the copy is deleted from their phone.
